# Security Policy

HASHI is a security-sensitive specification: it deals with identity keys, delegation and trust between people.

## Reporting a Problem

If you find a flaw in the specification that could let someone impersonate a person, gain trust they weren't given, or get around a revocation, please **don't open a public issue**.

Instead, report it privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on this repository's Security tab.

Please include:

- the section of the specification involved;
- a concrete scenario showing what goes wrong; and
- any fix you'd suggest.

## Scope

This policy covers the specification and its OpenAPI description. Problems in a particular implementation should be reported to that implementation's maintainers.
